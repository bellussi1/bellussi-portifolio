ATLAS

History as a navigable network — with a source for every claim.

ATLAS is an interactive encyclopedia of history, politics and society. People, works, concepts and events form a typed knowledge graph, and every statement carries its source plus whether it is documented fact, interpretation, controversy or an acknowledged gap.
